= Example Performance Review Template & Framework

When an employer does not provide a standardized internal performance review form this can be used.

== Key Sections of a Standard Performance Review

. *Employee & Review Information* -- Establishes review boundaries, role expectations, and assessment timeframes.
. *Core Responsibilities & Accomplishments* -- Focuses on concrete deliverables, key milestones, and business impacts achieved during the review period.
. *Competency Evaluation* -- Rates quantitative and qualitative behaviors (communication, execution, problem-solving, initiative).
. *Strengths & Areas for Growth* -- Provides targeted praise and identifies specific, actionable opportunities for performance enhancement.
. *SMART Goals & Objectives* -- Sets clear, measurable, and time-bound goals for the upcoming review period.
. *Professional Development Plan* -- Defines concrete training, resources, and managerial support required to foster growth.
. *Employee Self-Evaluation & Comments* -- Provides a formal space for employee perspective, self-reflection, and feedback.
. *Sign-Off & Acknowledgments* -- Captures formal acknowledgment from both parties that the evaluation took place.

== Rating Scale Reference

[cols="1,2,4", options="header"]
|===
| Rating | Descriptor | Definition

| 5
| Exceeds Expectations
| Consistently surpasses objectives and standards; serves as a benchmark for excellence.

| 4
| Meets Expectations
| Consistently satisfies all primary duties, expectations, and established criteria.

| 3
| Needs Improvement
| Partially meets requirements; inconsistent results warrant targeted adjustments.

| 2
| Unsatisfactory
| Consistently falls below minimum job requirements; immediate action required.
|===
